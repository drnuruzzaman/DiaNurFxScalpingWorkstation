5. WHERE CONDITIONS MOVE THE ODDS - a preview of the conditional tables (Phase C)
   LIFT = (realised - baseline) in the group, minus the same miss over ALL rows of that
   year - so a drift that lifts every group at once (a bull year, cheaper trades) is
   taken out and only what the condition adds is left. Percentage points. Listed only
   when the lift has the SAME sign in all three years AND its 90% interval excludes 0.
   A lift is a candidate, not a forecast: Phase C has to turn it into one and beat the
   baseline out of sample, with a penalty for how many candidates were looked at.
   34 of 288 condition groups met both tests. By chance alone about 22 would
   (90% range 14-29); more than that suggests some structure is real, but not WHICH rows.
