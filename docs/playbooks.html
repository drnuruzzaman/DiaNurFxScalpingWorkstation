Rangewhat price does inside and at the edges of a range - 9 playbooks

Range edge fade

range_edge_fade

Sell near the top of a range, buy near the bottom - but only after the edge visibly pushes price back.

When
Price is in the outer 18% of the range it is trading in (2.5+ ATR wide) and the bar rejected that edge. Horizontal: either edge. Ascending: buy the lower line only. Descending: sell the upper line only. Contracting: either edge. Expanding: only after a sweep through the line.
Entry
Market, at the rejection bar's close.
Stop
0.45 ATR beyond the edge (or the bar's wick, if further).
Target
Horizontal / ascending / descending: just inside the opposite edge. Contracting / expanding: the middle.

The range type matters: in a rising range only buy the floor, in a falling range only sell the ceiling.

False break

false_break

Price breaks out of a range or through a strong level, but cannot stay out - the breakout traders are trapped. Fade them.

When
A range edge or a strong S/R level (score 55+, within 4 ATR) broke on two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR beyond it, then closed back inside within 3 bars. Signals on the bar that closed back.
Entry
Market, at the close back inside, against the failed break.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the furthest point of the failed break.
Target
The other side of the range; for a level, the next strong level the other way.

Two closes outside then back in is the trap. One wick outside is a sweep (see Range sweep).

Range breakout

range_breakout

A range finally gives way. When two candles in a row close outside it, ride the move out of the box.

When
A range the chart draws (horizontal box) is broken by two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR beyond its edge; the signal is the second close.
Entry
Market, at the second close outside.
Stop
The middle of the range.
Target
A measured move: the range's height added to the broken edge.

If price closes back inside within 3 bars it was a false break - the stop at mid-range keeps that loss small.

Range retest

range_retest

After a range breaks, price often comes back to touch the edge it broke. The old ceiling now acts as a floor - buy that touch.

When
A range broke out 3-20 bars ago; the last bar came back to the broken edge (within 0.3 ATR) and closed back on the breakout side, in the breakout's colour.
Entry
Market, at the retest bar's close.
Stop
0.45 ATR back inside the range, beyond the retest wick.
Target
A measured move: the range's height from the broken edge.

The retest is the second chance for those who missed the breakout - and the stop is much tighter than chasing.

Range sweep

range_sweep

One candle's wick pokes through the range edge - grabbing the stop orders waiting there - but the candle closes back inside. Fade it.

When
A horizontal range (2+ ATR wide); the last bar's wick went more than 0.1 ATR through an edge and the bar closed back inside, in the opposite colour.
Entry
Market, at the sweep bar's close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the sweep's wick.
Target
Just inside the opposite edge.

A sweep is ONE wick. If price closes outside twice it is a breakout attempt instead.

Mid-range rotation

mid_range_rotation

In a wide range, price travels from one edge to the other. Once it bounces off one edge and crosses the middle, join the trip to the far side.

When
A horizontal range at least 4 ATR wide; in the last 8 bars price tested one edge (outer 20%), and the last bar closed through the midline.
Entry
Market, at the close through the midline.
Stop
Beyond the quarter line on the side price came from.
Target
Just inside the far edge.

Works best in wide, well-tested ranges. In narrow ranges the midline is too close to both edges.

Range compression breakout

range_compression_breakout

Inside a range the candles get smaller and smaller - the market is coiling. The first strong candle out of the coil often starts a fast move.

When
A horizontal or contracting range whose last 10 bars average under 65% of the bar size early in the range (or any contracting range); the last bar closes 0.3 ATR beyond the 10-bar coil with a body 60%+ of its range.
Entry
Market, at the breakout close.
Stop
0.2 ATR beyond the other side of the coil.
Target
The range's width from the entry.

Shrinking candles = energy building up. The breakout candle should be big and solid, not a wick.

Range expansion

range_expansion

A range whose swings keep getting bigger (a broadening range). When one of its sloping edges breaks on two closes, follow the expansion.

When
The range structure is EXPANDING (width grew 25%+); two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR beyond its upper or lower line.
Entry
Market, at the second close beyond the line.
Stop
0.6 ATR back inside the line.
Target
Half the range's current width beyond the line.

Expanding ranges whipsaw - this is the most aggressive range setup. Size down.

Range failure

range_failure

A range broke out properly, but 4-15 bars later price is back inside and through the middle. The breakout failed - trade toward the other edge.

When
A confirmed range breakout 4-15 bars ago; the last bar closed back through the range's midline.
Entry
Market, at the close through the midline.
Stop
Halfway between the midline and the broken edge, plus 0.3 ATR.
Target
The opposite edge.

Different from a false break: here the breakout was confirmed first, then gave up completely.

Breakoutprice leaves a structure and keeps going - 12 playbooks

Breakout retest

breakout_retest

A level breaks on two closes; price comes back to it, and the old level holds from the other side.

When
A level was broken on two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR beyond it; THIS bar retested the level and closed back on the breakout side, within 0.5 ATR of it. A trend only adds confidence when it points the break's way (against it is a penalty); a break bar over 3 ATR (a spike) counts against; a range regime counts against.
Entry
Market, at the retest bar's close - never later, never more than 0.5 ATR from the level.
Stop
0.35 ATR back through the broken level.
Target
The next strong level (score 55+) beyond.

Rebuilt 2026-11-08 after two lab losses: it used to enter before the break was confirmed, chase up to 5 bars after the retest, and score an uptrend as support for a sell.

Horizontal resistance breakout

hz_resistance_breakout

A ceiling that stopped price several times finally breaks. Two closes above it confirm buyers are in control.

When
A strong resistance or flip level (score 55+) gets two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR above it, the bar before them still below.
Entry
Market, at the second close above.
Stop
0.5 ATR below the level.
Target
The next level above.

The more times a level was tested, the more stop orders sit above it - and the stronger the break.

Horizontal support breakdown

hz_support_breakdown

A floor that held price several times finally breaks. Two closes below it confirm sellers are in control.

When
A strong support or flip level (score 55+) gets two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR below it, the bar before them still above.
Entry
Market, at the second close below.
Stop
0.5 ATR above the level.
Target
The next level below.

Mirror of the resistance breakout. Broken support often becomes resistance on the way back.

Trendline breakout

trendline_breakout

A trendline that held for a while is broken against its slope - a falling resistance line broken upward, or a rising support line broken downward. The trend may be changing.

When
A trendline with 3+ touches; two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR on the other side of it, the bar before them still on its side.
Entry
Market, at the second close beyond the line.
Stop
0.5 ATR back across the line.
Target
The price where the line started (the swing it was drawn from).

A line with more touches carries more weight. One touch is not a trendline.

Chart pattern breakout

pattern_break

A classic chart pattern reaches its trigger - trade the break, while it is fresh.

When
A classic pattern (head and shoulders, double/triple top or bottom, triangle, wedge, flag) reaches its trigger. Reversal patterns must follow the trend they reverse and have distinct, spaced points. A confirmed pattern is only traded within 5 bars of its break, and not once price has already reached its target.
Entry
Stop order at the break level while forming; market once the break is confirmed.
Stop
0.20 ATR beyond the pattern's invalidation point - no trade if that is more than 4 ATR from the entry.
Target
The pattern's measured move.

Tightened 2026-11-08 after a lab loss on a stale inverse head and shoulders read inside an uptrend (8.4 ATR stop). Measured on gold 1h Jun 2025 - Nov 2026 it is still about 50/50 to +1R - no edge yet.

Volatility squeeze breakout

squeeze_breakout

Bollinger Bands pinch together when the market goes quiet. Quiet does not last - the first strong candle out of the squeeze often runs.

When
On the bar before, the Bollinger width (20, 2) was in its lowest 20% of the last 120 bars; the last bar closes beyond the 20-bar high or low with a body 55%+ of its range.
Entry
Market, at the breakout close.
Stop
The middle of the 20-bar squeeze box.
Target
The squeeze box's height from the entry.

The squeeze tells you a move is coming, not which way - wait for the candle to choose.

News-driven breakout

news_breakout

A major US release (NFP, CPI, FOMC...) hits. Trade the first clean close out of the range price held just before the news.

When
Within 90 minutes after a MAJOR US release (NFP, CPI, FOMC, PPI, GDP, PCE, retail sales); the first close more than 0.3 ATR beyond the high / low of the 6 bars before the release.
Entry
Market, at that first close outside.
Stop
The lower of the low since the release and the pre-release midpoint (the higher of the two for a sell).
Target
2R (no structural target).

Spreads widen and price can whip both ways around news. Only major releases count here.

Momentum / displacement breakout

displacement_breakout

One huge, solid candle that blasts through the recent highs or lows. Big money just moved - follow it.

When
The last bar's body is at least 1.5 ATR and at least 70% of its range, and it closed beyond the last 10 bars' high (low).
Entry
Market, at the displacement candle's close.
Stop
The candle's body midpoint, minus 0.2 ATR.
Target
The next level beyond.

Look for a candle that is clearly bigger than its neighbours with little wick.

Liquidity sweep breakout

liquidity_sweep_breakout

Price first dips below recent lows to take the stops there, then reverses and breaks the high of that move. The sweep was fuel for a move the other way.

When
A liquidity sweep 1-10 bars ago (the most recent one); the last bar is the FIRST strong close (body 60%+ of range) beyond the other side of the move since that sweep.
Entry
Market, at the breakout close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the sweep's extreme.
Target
The strongest liquidity pool ahead.

Classic smart-money sequence: grab the stops on one side, then run to the other side.

Break of structure (BOS)

bos_breakout

Early in a new move, each new high breaks the last one (a BOS). Join on the first or second BOS - not the fourth.

When
The last bar is a BOS (a close through the latest swing in the trend's direction), and it is only the 1st or 2nd BOS since the last CHoCH; the current leg has run no more than 2.5 ATR; and the protected swing is within 3 ATR of the entry.
Entry
Market, at the BOS close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the protected swing (the latest swing low in an uptrend) - no trade if that is more than 3 ATR away.
Target
The strongest liquidity pool ahead.

Tightened 2026-11-08 after two lab losses: a 4th BOS at the top of a 3.5 ATR rally, and a BOS at the bottom of a drop with a 6.6 ATR stop. Late BOS = the move is running out.

Change of character (CHoCH)

choch_breakout

A downtrend makes lower highs. When price closes above the last protected high, the character changes - the trend may be turning up.

When
The last bar is a CHoCH (a close through the protected level against the trend).
Entry
Market, at the CHoCH close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the extreme of the leg that made the CHoCH.
Target
The strongest liquidity pool ahead.

A first CHoCH is often retested. Waiting for the retest (CHoCH reversal) gives a tighter stop.

FVG / order block breakout

fvg_ob_breakout

A supply zone (bearish order block or fair value gap) should push price down. When price closes clean through it instead, the sellers there failed.

When
The last bar closed more than 0.2 ATR beyond an OPPOSING fair value gap or order block (as it stood one bar earlier), the bar before still inside or short of it.
Entry
Market, at the close through the zone.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the far side of the zone.
Target
The next level beyond.

A zone that fails often flips: broken supply becomes demand.

Reversalthe move loses control and turns - 9 playbooks

Liquidity sweep reversal

liquidity_sweep_reversal

A wick runs the stops beyond equal highs / lows and closes back - nobody followed through. Fade it, but only with room to run.

When
A confirmed sweep at most 3 bars ago; price still within 1 ATR of the swept level; a CHoCH or a rejection wick after the sweep; higher timeframes not strongly against (score 50+ the other way blocks it); and the first opposing order block, FVG, supply/demand zone or strong level at least 1R away (with the final stop).
Entry
Market, at the signal bar's close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the sweep's extreme (widened to the noise floor if needed).
Target
The strongest liquidity pool on the other side.

Rebuilt 2026-11-08 after two lab losses that reached +0.7R and reversed at a bearish order block: the room rule is what keeps TP1 out of the opposing zone.

CHoCH reversal

choch_reversal

Stops are swept, then a CHoCH confirms the turn. Instead of chasing the CHoCH, wait for price to come back to the broken level and buy the retest.

When
A CHoCH 1-12 bars ago that came within 20 bars after a liquidity sweep the same way; the last bar retested the CHoCH level (within 0.3 ATR) and closed back beyond it.
Entry
Market, at the retest close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the reversal's extreme.
Target
The strongest liquidity pool ahead.

Sweep -> CHoCH -> retest is the full smart-money reversal sequence.

Order block reversal

ob_reversal

An order block is the last opposite candle before a big move. When price returns to the block that STARTED a reversal (a CHoCH), it often bounces again.

When
Price dips into a bullish order block (made by a CHoCH, not yet fully mitigated) and closes back above it, bullish (mirror for bearish).
Entry
Market, at the close back out of the block.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the block (or the wick, if further).
Target
The latest swing high (low).

Only order blocks that created a CHoCH count here - those mark where a reversal began.

Fair value gap (FVG) reversal

fvg_reversal

A fast reversal leaves a gap where only one side traded (an FVG). Price tends to come back to fill it - and the gap often holds.

When
After a CHoCH, price dips into the fair value gap the reversal left and closes back above it, bullish (mirror for bearish).
Entry
Market, at the close back out of the gap.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the gap (or the wick).
Target
The latest swing high (low).

FVG = three candles where the 1st and 3rd do not overlap. The space between is the gap.

Engulfing reversal

engulfing_reversal

After a drop into support, a green candle swallows the whole previous red body. Buyers took over within one bar.

When
The last body engulfs the previous opposite body; price fell (rose) 1+ ATR over the 5 bars before; and the two bars sit at a strong level, a demand (supply) zone or a swept liquidity pool.
Entry
Market, at the engulfing close.
Stop
0.2 ATR beyond the two-bar extreme.
Target
The next level.

Location is everything - an engulfing candle in the middle of nowhere means little.

Rejection wick reversal

rejection_wick_reversal

A pin bar: price shoots down, gets rejected, and closes near the top. The long wick shows the level refused lower prices.

When
The lower wick is at least 2x the body and 60%+ of the bar, the close in the top third, the bar 0.6+ ATR long; after a 0.8+ ATR move into a strong level, zone or swept pool (mirror for an upper wick).
Entry
Market, at the pin bar's close.
Stop
0.2 ATR beyond the wick.
Target
The next level.

The longer the wick compared with the body, the stronger the rejection.

Three-candle reversal

three_candle_reversal

Morning star: a big red candle, a tiny indecision candle, then a big green candle closing past the middle of the red one.

When
Bar 1 body 0.8+ ATR one way; bar 2 body no more than 35% of bar 1's; bar 3 the other colour, closing past bar 1's midpoint. A level / zone nearby adds confidence.
Entry
Market, at the third candle's close.
Stop
0.2 ATR beyond the pattern's extreme.
Target
The next level.

Evening star is the bearish mirror at the top of a move.

Double top / double bottom

double_top_bottom

Price tests the same high twice and fails both times (double top). When it closes below the low between the two peaks (the neckline), the reversal is confirmed.

When
A double top (bottom) the engine detected, ended within 30 bars; the last bar is the FIRST close through its neckline.
Entry
Market, at the neckline close.
Stop
0.2 ATR beyond the pattern's invalidation (the higher peak / lower trough).
Target
The pattern's measured move.

Double top = bearish reversal; double bottom = bullish reversal.

Divergence reversal

divergence_reversal

Price makes a lower low but RSI makes a higher low - selling momentum is fading. Buy once a candle confirms by closing above the previous bar's high.

When
Regular RSI divergence whose second pivot is 1-5 bars back; the last bar closes beyond the previous bar's high (bullish) or low (bearish).
Entry
Market, at the confirmation close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the extreme since the divergence pivot.
Target
The next level.

Divergence is a warning, not a signal on its own - always wait for the confirmation candle.

Pullbackthe trend stays intact and price comes back to it - 9 playbooks

Trendline bounce

trendline_bounce

A rejection off the leg's trend line.

When
A rising support (or falling resistance) line with 3+ touches; the last bar tagged it and closed back off it, with the trend.
Entry
Market, at the signal bar's close.
Stop
0.50 ATR beyond the line.
Target
The opposite line of the leg.

Original playbook.

Trend structure pullback

trend_structure_pullback

An uptrend makes higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL). When price pulls back to the last higher low and bounces, the trend is intact - buy.

When
The swing trend is up (down); the last bar came down to the last HL (LH) - within 0.5 ATR above it and no more than 0.3 ATR below - and closed back above it, bullish.
Entry
Market, at the bounce close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the HL (or the wick).
Target
The last HH (LL).

If price closes below the last higher low, the uptrend structure is broken - that is the invalidation.

BOS retest pullback

bos_retest_pullback

After a break of structure, the broken high usually gets retested. Buy when price comes back to it and holds.

When
A BOS 3-20 bars ago; the last bar came back to the broken level (within 0.3 ATR) and closed back beyond it, in the trade's colour.
Entry
Market, at the retest close.
Stop
0.5 ATR back through the level.
Target
The extreme since the BOS.

Same idea as a range retest, but the level is the swing that the BOS broke.

Internal structure pullback

internal_structure_pullback

During a pullback in an uptrend, price makes its own small lower highs. When it breaks the last of those small highs, the pullback is over.

When
Swing trend up (down); the pullback (last 15 bars) is at least 1 ATR deep and its last internal 3-bar swing high (low) is closed through on the last bar.
Entry
Market, at the close through the internal swing.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the pullback's extreme.
Target
The high (low) before the pullback.

Think of it as a mini CHoCH inside the pullback, back in the trend's direction.

Fair value gap (FVG) pullback

fvg_pullback

A strong trend leg leaves a fair value gap. When price pulls back into the gap and bounces, join the trend.

When
After a BOS, price dips into the fair value gap the impulse left and closes back above it, bullish (mirror for bearish).
Entry
Market, at the close back out of the gap.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the gap (or the wick).
Target
The latest swing high (low).

Same mechanics as the FVG reversal - but here the gap comes from a BOS (with the trend).

Order block pullback

ob_pullback

The last down candle before a BOS rally is a bullish order block. Price often returns to it - buy the bounce.

When
After a BOS, price dips into the order block the BOS came from (not yet fully mitigated) and closes back above it, bullish (mirror for bearish).
Entry
Market, at the close back out of the block.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the block (or the wick).
Target
The latest swing high (low).

Same mechanics as the order block reversal - but here the block comes from a BOS (with the trend).

Fibonacci pullback

fibonacci_pullback

Trends often pull back 61.8%-78.6% of the last leg before continuing. Buy a rejection inside that 'golden pocket'.

When
Swing trend up (down); the last bar reached the 61.8-78.6% retracement pocket of the last leg and closed in its upper half, bullish.
Entry
Market, at the rejection close.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the leg's start.
Target
The leg's extreme.

If price goes past 100% (the leg's start), the leg is fully undone - that is the stop.

Moving average pullback

ma_pullback

In a clean trend the fast EMA rides above the slow EMA. Price dipping to the fast EMA and bouncing is a with-trend entry.

When
Fast EMA (21) above the slow EMA (55), both rising over 5 bars; the last bar's low touched the fast EMA and it closed back above it (and above the slow EMA), bullish.
Entry
Market, at the bounce close.
Stop
0.3 ATR below the lower of the bar's low and the slow EMA.
Target
The 20-bar high (low).

A close below the slow EMA means the trend is weakening - that is the invalidation.

Volatility pullback

volatility_pullback

The pullback is quiet: candles shrink and the narrowest bar of four appears (NR4). When price breaks that quiet bar in the trend's direction, the trend resumes.

When
Swing trend up (down); the last 5 bars' average range is under 70% of the 20 before; the bar before the last is the narrowest of 4; the last bar closes beyond its high (low), bullish.
Entry
Market, at the close past the NR4 bar.
Stop
0.30 ATR beyond the last 6 bars' extreme.
Target
The 26-bar high (low).

Quiet pullbacks mean nobody is selling hard - the trend has energy left.

Rules every playbook shares

  • Closed bars only. A setup is judged when its bar closes; a market entry is at that close.
  • TP1 is 1R. TP2 is the playbook's own target when it is between 1.2R and 6R away, otherwise 2R.
  • Stops sit beyond structure (a swing, a level, a zone, a wick) plus a small ATR pad, then are widened to the noise floor (Risk & gates: minimum stop in ATR) if too tight.
  • The exit follows Risk & gates: by default, at TP1 the stop locks in a small profit and then trails by 1 ATR on closed bars.
  • A signal is not a trade. Every signal still passes the gates (confidence, spread, ATR cost, news, daily limits, room to target) before it can be sent.
  • On / off is per playbook in Settings → Playbooks. New playbooks start OFF and have not been backtested - test one in the Backtest lab before switching it on for live.

Glossary

ATR (Average True Range)

The average size of a candle over the last 14 bars. Stops, pads and distances are measured in ATR so the same rule works in quiet and busy markets.

R (risk unit)

The distance from entry to stop. A trade that makes 2R made twice what it risked.

Swing high / swing low

A peak (or trough) with lower highs (or higher lows) on both sides. The building block of market structure.

HH, HL, LH, LL

Higher high, higher low (an uptrend); lower high, lower low (a downtrend).

BOS - break of structure

A close through the latest swing in the trend's direction: the trend continues.

CHoCH - change of character

A close through the protected level AGAINST the trend: the first sign the trend may be turning.

Protected level

The swing a trend must not break: in an uptrend, the low the last rally started from.

Liquidity

Clusters of stop orders, usually just beyond equal highs / lows and obvious swings.

Sweep

A wick runs through liquidity and the candle closes back - the stops were taken but price did not stay.

Order block (OB)

The last opposite candle before a move that broke structure - where large orders are thought to have been placed.

Fair value gap (FVG)

Three candles where the first and third do not overlap: a gap only one side traded through. Price often returns to it.

Two-close breakout

The rule every breakout here uses: two closes in a row more than 0.3 ATR beyond the level. One close, or a wick, is not enough.

Retest

After a breakout, price comes back to the level it broke. Broken resistance often acts as support.

Range types

Horizontal (flat), ascending (both edges rising), descending (both falling), contracting (narrowing) and expanding (widening).

EMA

Exponential moving average. Here the fast one is 21 bars and the slow one 55.

Bollinger squeeze

Bollinger Bands (20, 2) unusually narrow: the market is quiet, which usually ends in a sharp move.

RSI divergence

Price makes a new extreme but RSI does not - the move is losing momentum.

NR4

Narrow range 4: the smallest candle of the last four. A sign of a pause before the next move.
